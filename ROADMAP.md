# Roadmap

Validated on 2026-11-20. Each step closes on the owner's decision; no code before the roadmap and the mockup were
validated (done).

1. **Cover poll** (72 hours): three covers, one direction chosen.
2. **Title check**: registries, stores, portals, domain and handles, before any public use of the title.
3. **Roadmap and mockup**: validated on 2026-11-20.
4. **Prototype**: one desert arena and its first minute; the falling-sand engine scaled up; the truck sprites and a
   first set of hero sprites; a session log with no personal data.
   Done when 8 strangers out of 10 still play at 60 seconds, alone; the game starts in under 10 seconds on a
   simulated mid-range phone; the world size the engine sustains is measured.
5. **Vertical slice**: the desert region at final quality, the mutation that opens the lagoons, two colony buildings.
6. **Production of v1**: two regions, the colony (one screen, six buildings, three levels each), five glass
   mutations, the threat level and the two human responses (investigation while the hero stays unseen, strategies
   against the colony once he is seen).
7. **Media**: final covers from the game, the hover video, clips.
8. **Portal builds and one submission**: translations, saves, SDKs and builds only after the final version is
   approved; Playgama first, then one CrazyGames submission on proof.

After acceptance: the alternative wave mode that brings the threat back down, and room for many more powers,
upgrades and enemies, with the colony at the centre.
